# PitSignal

Project by **Kintounn**. Logo generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project.

The horizontal mark combines a racing corner with telemetry signal arcs, violet/cyan accents and white lettering for a dark dashboard. Use `pitsignal-logo.png` on a dark background and preserve its transparency and aspect ratio.

Design prompt: “Create a professional horizontal logo for PitSignal, an open-source racing telemetry dashboard intended for multiple racing simulation games. Exact wordmark: PitSignal. Compact racing apex and telemetry signal emblem on the left, bold forward-leaning sans-serif lettering on the right. White lettering for a black dashboard, violet and cyan emblem accents, transparent background, legible at small sizes. One logo, no tagline, no F1 branding, no mockup.”
